Before we begin

Questions

What photographs should I send?

For a five-page edition, send 8–15 clear, high-resolution photographs. Ten-page editions usually need 15–30. You must own the photographs or have permission to use them.

How long does a magazine take?

Most editions are delivered within five business days after all photographs and answers arrive. A 48-hour option is available for suitable projects.

Is anything physically shipped?

The standard product is a high-resolution digital PDF. A print-ready option prepares the file for professional printing; physical printing will be offered separately.

Are children's photographs kept private?

Yes. A parent or guardian must approve every children's project. Photographs and finished pages are never used publicly without separate permission.

Do you use AI?

NEMO Muse Lite is a private guided story framework that works in your browser and does not upload your answers. AI-assisted artwork may be used later when a chosen concept benefits from it; the studio directs, edits and assembles every final design.
